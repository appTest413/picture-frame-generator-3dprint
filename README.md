# picture-frame-generator-3dprint

Parametric Picture Frame: 4×6, 5×7, 8×10, A4. Dovetail keys, stand, wall hanger

Description
A 3D-printed picture frame with a clean front and no visible joints.

Sizes: sized to your photo, with a flat front lip, a pocket for the photo and glass, and a back plate.
Big frames on small beds: frames larger than your bed split into pieces. They join with separate dovetail keys that press into pockets in the back, so the front shows no seams. Keys are printed separately.
Hanging: keyhole slots on the top and side edges, with the same spacing on each edge. A hinged kickstand leg is also included, held by a length of 1.75 mm filament.
Files: each size comes as a 3MF plate with parts already laid out, plus individual STL files.

What to print

Frame pieces (front face down for the flat style)
Back plate (inner face down)
Keys: print the quantity in the file name, for example key_x4
Kickstand leg, if you use it

Suggested starting settings (untested)

PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill
Keys: 100% infill or 4+ walls, and slow the outer wall
If a key is too tight or too loose, change the tolerance and reprint the keys

Assembly

Lay the frame pieces face down on a flat surface.
Press a key into the back pocket across each joint, wide face toward you.
Add a drop of glue if you want the joint permanent.
Put in the photo, then the back plate.
Hang it on a screw, or fit the kickstand.
